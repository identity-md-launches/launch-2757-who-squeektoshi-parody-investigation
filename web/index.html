What this alibi means

Running means fresh, hardware-reported wheel speed above zero. Resting means zero speed, not proven sleep. Stale means the reading is old or cannot reliably describe the wheel. Offline means this page could not retrieve status.

Freshness uses the API’s threshold (currently 120 seconds), response time, and observation and receipt timestamps. Device faults and non-hardware readings never establish an alibi. This is telemetry, not proof of identity.

Squeektoshi is a hamster.

The answer was in the hamster documentation.

The project identifies a male Syrian hamster. The API records wheel activity; Ethereum records authorized settlements. The species conclusion follows the biography, not a cryptographic proof of hamsterhood.

Still open: the two core histories, the reward-policy conflict and independent verification of physical measurements. None requires investigating people.
